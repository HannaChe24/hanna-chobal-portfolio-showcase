ABOUT ME.

I’m Hanna, a Senior Product Designer focused on complex SaaS, fintech, data and operational products. I work where dense information, business rules and multiple user roles need to become one coherent experience.

My contribution goes beyond individual screens: I clarify the product model, expose trade-offs, align decisions with product and engineering, and translate them into flows, states and reusable patterns that can be built and maintained.

My portfolio includes production work from my mid-level years in 2021–2023, alongside more recent conceptual explorations. In both, I focus on the decisions behind the interface: what needed to change, why the chosen structure worked and how the product became easier to use or scale.
